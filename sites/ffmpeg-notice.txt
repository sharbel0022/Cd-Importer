TON uses FFmpeg WebAssembly for browser audio conversion.

@ffmpeg/ffmpeg 0.12.15 - MIT
@ffmpeg/core 0.12.10 (single-thread) - GPL-2.0-or-later

The FFmpeg core is redistributed without modification. Its binary is split
for transport and reassembled unchanged; the manifest contains its SHA-256.
